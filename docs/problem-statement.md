# Problem Statement
Client: LetsGo, a travel agency running buses from Buea park to Douala, with a stop in Douala. Passengers book at two desks, Mile 17 and Douala Garoutier, using paper registers.

Problems:
1. The same seat is sometimes sold to two passengers, or more tickets are sold than the bus has seats. It is only discovered when passengers arrive to board.
2. Nearly empty departures go unnoticed until the bus is about to leave, so the agency cannot merge or cancel them in time.
3. Parcels get lost and the agency cannot prove what was received, from whom, or whether it was delivered.
4. Booking for a departure never closes, so passengers are still booked after the bus has left.
5. The two desks keep separate records, so neither can see what the other has sold.
